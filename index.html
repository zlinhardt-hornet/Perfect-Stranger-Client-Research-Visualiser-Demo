Format guide

Structure:

Company Name (TICKER) — Status
One-line mission or tagline

Section Header:
- A bullet. Becomes a card. (Source: press)
- Label: bullets starting with "Word: " control the card title.

Leadership:
- Name — Title — CONFIDENCE — Source
- * Name — Title — CONFIDENCE — Source | photo: https://...

Decision:
- The headline verdict sentence
- A reasoning bullet
- Another reasoning bullet

Next Steps:
- A next step
- Another next step
  • Any section header becomes a Milanote-style column, except Leadership, Decision / Recommendation, Next Steps, and About / Overview / Company, which populate the dedicated boxes instead.
  • Prefix a leadership bullet with * to flag it as a target contact — it's highlighted in yellow on the board.
  • Confidence must contain the word high, medium, low, or unverified — anything else defaults to medium.
  • The decision verdict's color is picked up from words like "pursue" (green), "caution" / "maybe" (amber, default), or "pass" / "not a fit" (red).
  • Add (Source: ...) at the end of a card bullet to show a source tag on hover.
